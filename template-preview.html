3. Duration of Agreement & Cancellation Policy

In Plain English

Services renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle unless you submit a cancellation request through the Sheernox Client Portal before renewal. Domain registrations, SSL certificates, and custom setup fees are non-refundable.

3.1 Term and Auto-Renewal: Subscriptions automatically renew for recurring billing intervals (monthly, quarterly, or annually) at the prevailing rates unless cancelled in writing or via the client dashboard prior to the renewal date.

3.2 Cancellation Method: Cancellation requests must be submitted through the verified Sheernox Client Portal (my.sheernox.com). Email or phone cancellation requests require secondary verification to safeguard against unauthorized account shutdowns.

17. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Uptime Guarantee

In Plain English

We provide a 99.9% network and infrastructure uptime commitment. If we fail to meet this threshold in a given month, you are eligible for service credits on your next invoice, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

17.1 Sheernox Technology Group strives to maintain a 99.9% uptime metric across cloud hypervisors, network transit, and core routing equipment.

17.2 Scheduled maintenance windows, emergency security patching, customer-side application errors, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) events exceeding purchased mitigation capacities are excluded from SLA outage calculations.
