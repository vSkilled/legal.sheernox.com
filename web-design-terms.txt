1.0 PROJECT LIFECYCLE & STAGING REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IN PLAIN ENGLISH]
Projects follow a structured, collaborative lifecycle: Discovery → Design &
Prototyping → Development → Staging QA → Production Launch. You review working
builds on a secure staging server before public release.

Upon execution of a Statement of Work and receipt of the required initiation
deposit, Sheernox schedules and executes the project through the following
progressive lifecycle:

1.1 Discovery & Technical Architecture:
    Sheernox collaborates with Client to define project goals, user journeys,
    functional specifications, content requirements, and technical architecture
    documented in the project brief or SOW.

1.2 UI/UX Concepts & Prototyping:
    Sheernox produces interactive wireframes, component design systems, or
    visual design concepts for Client review and aesthetic alignment.

1.3 Engineering & Integration:
    Following design milestone sign-off, Sheernox builds the frontend
    interfaces, server-side logic, database schemas, third-party API connectors,
    and content structures.

1.4 Staging Review & Quality Assurance:
    Prior to public deployment, deliverables are assembled and tested on a
    secure, authenticated Sheernox staging subdomain for cross-device
    validation, performance auditing, and Client functional inspection.

1.5 Final Delivery & Public Deployment:
    Following final milestone acceptance and settlement of project invoices,
    Sheernox coordinates DNS cutover, SSL certificate provisioning, and
    production deployment.
