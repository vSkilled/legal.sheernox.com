CRITICAL POLICY WARNING

Misuse, abuse, or unauthorized exploitation of Sheernox systems will result in immediate suspension or permanent termination of service without prior notice. Each customer is legally responsible for all activities occurring under their accounts.

Preamble & Policy Scope

In Plain English

This policy protects all clients and infrastructure from harmful activities. By utilizing any Sheernox service—including shared hosting, VPS, dedicated servers, domain management, email, CDN, or backup storage—you agree to abide by these rules.

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the standards designed to protect Sheernox Technology Group, its included subsidiaries, cloud hypervisors, IP transit networks, and all persons using Sheernox products and services (collectively, "Customers") from disruption, performance degradation, and malicious exploitation.

Each Customer’s use of Sheernox Technology Group’s products, services, networks, hardware, and IP space constitutes continuous acceptance of the Acceptable Use Policy in effect at the time of use.

#1 – Unacceptable Usage

In Plain English

You cannot use Sheernox to host or transmit illegal content, unlicensed software, harassment, hate speech, defamation, fraudulent schemes, or deceptive marketing.

Every customer is accountable for ensuring that any systems, applications, containers, and websites hosted with Sheernox are not knowingly or negligently employed for the activities listed below:

  1. The posting, transmission, caching, or storage of material that violates any local, state, federal, or international law, statute, or regulation.
  2. Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploitation of minors. Any detection will result in immediate termination and referral to law enforcement agencies (including NCMEC).
  3. Harassing, defamatory, threatening, obscene, or hateful transmissions designed to intimidate individuals or entities.
  4. The distribution of unlicensed, cracked, or pirated software (warez), proprietary game server emulators, or unauthorized licensing bypass tools.
  5. Deceptive marketing practices, Ponzi schemes, advance fee fraud, counterfeit goods marketing, or phishing credential harvesters.
  6. Operating unauthorized proxy, Tor exit nodes, or open relay networks that route malicious traffic through Sheernox IP ranges.

#2 – Prohibited Security Attacks & Network Exploits

In Plain English

Do not attempt to compromise server security, launch DDoS attacks, run unauthorized port scanners, distribute malware, or run cryptocurrency mining software on our infrastructure.

2.1 System Penetration & Scanning: Performing port scans, vulnerability probes, brute-force password attacks, or stress tests against Sheernox infrastructure or third-party networks without prior written authorization is strictly forbidden.

2.2 Botnets & DDoS Participation: Operating Command and Control (C2) nodes, IRC bots, amplification vectors, or participating in Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks will result in immediate network isolation.

2.3 Cryptocurrency Mining: Running CPU, GPU, or I/O-intensive cryptocurrency mining algorithms (e.g., Monero, Bitcoin, Chia) is strictly prohibited on all Shared, Reseller, and standard Cloud VPS instances.

#3 – Email & Anti-Spam Zero Tolerance

In Plain English

Sheernox enforces an absolute zero-tolerance anti-spam policy. You cannot send unsolicited mass email or purchased contact lists. If an account sends spam, it is suspended immediately to safeguard IP reputation.

3.1 Unsolicited Bulk Email (UBE): Sending unsolicited mass commercial email, purchased mailing lists, automated email harvesting, or utilizing open mail relays is strictly prohibited under the CAN-SPAM Act, CASL, and GDPR regulations.

3.2 Confirmed Opt-In Required: All mailing lists operated on Sheernox infrastructure must employ double opt-in verification and include functioning one-click unsubscribe links.

3.3 Hourly Outbound Thresholds: Shared and Reseller hosting plans are subject to an outbound transmission ceiling of 500 emails per hour per domain to preserve shared mail server delivery scores.

#4 – Hosting Resource Usage Limits

In Plain English

Shared hosting customers share server CPU, memory, and disk I/O. Scripts cannot run continuously or consume excessive server power that disrupts other customers. VPS and dedicated servers must maintain patched operating systems.

4.1 Shared & Reseller Compute Limits: Accounts on shared infrastructure may not consume more than 25% of server CPU resources or 1024MB RAM continuously for longer than 90 seconds. Automated cron jobs must not be scheduled at intervals shorter than 15 minutes.

4.2 Database Health: MySQL/MariaDB queries executing longer than 30 seconds or queries requiring temporary disk tables exceeding 2GB may be killed automatically to prevent hypervisor lockup.

4.3 VPS & Dedicated Server Security: Clients with root access are responsible for OS hardening, firewall rules, and prompt installation of security updates. If an unmanaged VPS is compromised, Sheernox reserves the right to isolate the virtual machine until remediated.

#5 – Bandwidth, CDN & Storage Fair Use

In Plain English

Storage allocated to web hosting accounts must be used for active website files, databases, and emails directly associated with the hosted domain—not for file sharing, public file dumps, or pirated media archiving.

5.1 Storage Intent: Web hosting disk space is provided exclusively for legitimate website content, active web applications, and functional email. Accounts may not be repurposed as public file dumping vaults, personal desktop backups, or torrent distribution nodes.

5.2 CDN Fair Bandwidth: Sheernox Content Delivery Network (CDN) edge caching is designed for web assets, images, stylesheets, and scripts. Unmetered bandwidth provisions remain subject to fair operational use without intentional network saturation.

#6 – Automated AI Scraping & System Integrity

In Plain English

Customers hosting AI agents, automated scrapers, or crawler bots must respect robots.txt and rate limits, and may not target third-party websites without authorization.

6.1 Responsible Automation: Customers operating automated bots, web crawlers, AI scraping models, or indexing tools must comply with published robots.txt directives, maintain valid user-agent strings identifying the operator, and respect destination server rate-limits.

6.2 Evasion & Proxy Abuses: Utilizing rotating residential proxies or IP spoofing techniques to bypass third-party access controls from Sheernox servers is strictly prohibited.

#7 – Enforcement, Suspension & Remediation

7.1 Right to Monitor & Act: Sheernox reserves the right to monitor network traffic, system metrics, and logs to verify adherence to this policy. In urgent circumstances involving severe abuse or ongoing attacks, Sheernox may disable offending accounts or IP routes immediately.

7.2 Clean-Up & Delisting Fees: In the event of system compromise resulting in outbound spam, malware propagation, or blacklist placement, an administrative remediation fee of up to $250.00 USD may be assessed to cover operational labor.

#8 – Reporting Violations

In Plain English

To report abuse, spam, phishing, or copyright infringement originating from our network, contact our Security and Abuse Desk with complete logs and email headers.
