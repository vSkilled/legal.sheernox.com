Preamble & Agreement Scope

In Plain English

This agreement specifically governs custom branding, graphic design, user experience (UX) prototyping, website development, and ongoing website care & maintenance plans provided by Sheernox. It is maintained separately from hosting and infrastructure services.

By commissioning, ordering, or retaining Sheernox Technology Group ("Sheernox") for branding, graphic design, UI/UX architecture, custom software development, or ongoing website care, the customer ("Customer", "Client", or "You") confirms that You are bound by this specialized Web Design, Branding & Care Agreement.

This Agreement governs creative, front-end, and CMS maintenance engagements. Infrastructure hosting and network connectivity remain governed by the primary Master Terms and Conditions.

1. Project Workflow & Delivery Policy

In Plain English

Projects begin once the scope is finalized and initial deposit is received. We deliver initial concepts for your review, iterate based on written feedback, and test thoroughly on staging servers before public deployment.

1.1 Discovery & Scoping: Customer collaborates with a Sheernox technical consultant to scope project requirements, wireframe user flows, and establish project milestones documented in a Statement of Work (SOW).

1.2 Iterative Design Concepts: Sheernox produces initial design prototypes (interactive mockups or staging drafts) for Customer feedback.

1.3 Staging Server Testing: Prior to public release, websites are assembled and tested on a password-protected Sheernox staging subdomain for cross-device review and quality assurance.

2. Branding, Graphic Design & UX Architecture

2.1 Visual Assets: Logo design, brand style guides, typography systems, vector illustrations, and UX design components created by Sheernox are custom-tailored to the specifications outlined in the project brief.

2.2 File Deliverables: Upon final milestone payment, branding assets are delivered in standard industry formats (SVG, PDF, PNG, and layered master files).

3. Review, Feedback & Revision Thresholds

In Plain English

Each design milestone includes two (2) consolidated rounds of revisions. Feedback must be submitted in writing within ten (10) business days. Inactivity beyond 10 days constitutes milestone acceptance so projects stay on schedule.

3.1 Revision Rounds: Unless otherwise specified in an SOW, each design phase includes up to two (2) consolidated rounds of minor revisions to typography, color palette, layout spacing, and content positioning.

3.2 Scope Changes: Significant structural redesigns, architecture modifications, or feature requests exceeding the approved scope will be quoted under a Change Order at Sheernox’s standard hourly design rate.

3.3 10-Day Deemed Acceptance: Customer agrees to review deliverables and provide written feedback or sign-off within ten (10) business days of submission. Failure to provide feedback within ten (10) business days constitutes constructive milestone acceptance.

4. Website Care & Ongoing Maintenance Plans

In Plain English

Website Care plans cover ongoing CMS updates, security patching, uptime checks, and automated backups. Maintenance retainers do not include major visual overhauls or third-party API rebuilds unless explicitly scoped.

4.1 Maintenance Scope: Sheernox Website Care packages include scheduled CMS core updates (e.g. WordPress), security patch deployment, premium plugin updates, daily cloud backups, and proactive uptime monitoring.

4.2 Out-of-Scope Requests: Ongoing maintenance retainers exclude new feature builds, third-party API deprecations, bespoke database migrations, or redesigns, which shall be billed separately.

5. Client Content & Third-Party Asset Licenses

5.1 Client Materials: Customer warrants that all copy, photographs, trademarks, and media furnished to Sheernox are owned by Customer or properly licensed. Customer defends and indemnifies Sheernox against any copyright or trademark claims arising from supplied assets.

5.2 Third-Party Licenses: Commercial stock photos, proprietary web fonts, and specialized commercial plugin licenses purchased on Customer’s behalf are registered to Customer, who remains responsible for recurring license renewals.

6. Copyright Transfer, Source Code Ownership & File Handoff

In Plain English

Once all project invoices are paid in full, full intellectual property and copyright of the custom design and code are transferred to you. Sheernox retains the right to display the completed work in its creative portfolio.

6.1 IP Transfer upon Full Payment: Conditioned upon full and final payment of all invoices, Sheernox transfers to Customer all right, title, and copyright in the bespoke visual layout, graphics, and custom CSS/HTML created for the project.

6.2 Pre-Existing Tools & Open Source: Open-source software (GPL, MIT, Apache libraries) and pre-existing Sheernox internal development frameworks remain subject to their respective licenses and are not transferred.

6.3 File Transfer & External Hosting Fee: When Customer elects to host their completed website on external third-party servers rather than Sheernox cloud infrastructure, an archival packaging and migration fee of $150.00 USD may apply for data bundling and DNS cutover support.

6.4 Portfolio Display: Sheernox reserves the professional right to showcase completed project designs, case studies, and live links in its online agency portfolio and marketing materials.

7. Cross-Browser & Device Compatibility

7.1 Supported Platforms: Sheernox designs websites to function consistently across current, evergreen versions of modern browsers (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge) and modern iOS/Android mobile screens. Compatibility with discontinued browsers (e.g. Internet Explorer) is expressly disclaimed.

8. Payment Milestones, Retainers & Cancellation

8.1 Milestone Schedule: Website projects typically require an initial non-refundable deposit (typically 50%) to initiate discovery, with subsequent milestone payments due upon completion of staging review and prior to public DNS launch.

8.2 Early Project Termination: In the event Customer cancels a project prior to completion, Sheernox retains the deposit and Customer shall be billed for all design and engineering hours completed up to the cancellation notice date.

9. Limitation of Liability & Survival

SHEERNOX TECHNOLOGY GROUP DISCLAIMS ANY LIABILITY FOR THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE BUGS, OPEN-SOURCE CMS VULNERABILITIES, CLIENT-SIDE DATA ENTRY ERRORS, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION ARISING FROM WEBSITE LAUNCH DELAYS. SHEERNOX’S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY FOR CREATIVE AND DEVELOPMENT SERVICES SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL PROJECT FEES RECEIVED UNDER THE APPLICABLE STATEMENT OF WORK. THIS SECTION AND SECTIONS 5, 6, AND 9 SHALL SURVIVE TERMINATION.
