Preamble & Contracting Capacity

In Plain English

This agreement specifically governs branding, graphic design, user interface prototyping, website engineering, and ongoing care retainers provided by Sheernox Technology Group, a sole proprietorship registered in Kamloops, BC, Canada. You must be at least 18 years of age to contract with us.

By placing an order, initiating a project deposit, or executing a Statement of Work with Sheernox Technology Group ("Sheernox", "We", "Us", or "Our"), a sole proprietorship registered in the City of Kamloops, Province of British Columbia, Canada, the customer ("Customer", "Client", or "You") confirms that You are in agreement with and legally bound by these Web Design Terms and Conditions.

Contracting Capacity & Order Formation: Sheernox carries out creative and web development services only for clients who are eighteen (18) years of age or older. An "order" is deemed to be a binding contract between Sheernox and the Customer upon receipt of written or verbal instructions, including agreements made via email, telephone, written Statement of Work, or electronic authorization through the Sheernox Client Portal (my.sheernox.com).

This specialized agreement operates in harmony with our Master Terms and Conditions and Acceptable Use Policy. Where hosting, DNS, or server infrastructure is bundled with a web project, those technical services are governed by the Master Terms and Conditions.

1. Project Workflow & Delivery Policy

In Plain English

Once time frames, budget, and the 50% initiation deposit are settled, we schedule your project. We scope your requirements, build rough concepts for your review, refine based on your itemized written feedback, and test on staging servers prior to release.

Once Sheernox and the Customer agree on a basic project timeframe and budget, and upon receipt of the initial deposit, Sheernox will schedule the project workflow and provide initial design concepts according to the following delivery policy:

1.1 Discovery & Scoping: Customer will collaborate with a Sheernox technical consultant to scope, define, and document the architectural, visual, and functional requirements of the website project.

1.2 Initial Drafts & Concepts: Sheernox will provide rough, initial drafts and interactive concepts of the website design for Customer inspection and review.

1.3 Structured Written Review: Customer agrees to review first design drafts and furnish a consolidated list of requested modifications clearly documented in writing (via email, shared project document, or formal review meeting).

1.4 Iterative Milestone Acceptance: Customer and Sheernox will meet or correspond at regular intervals to review progress. Customer reviews and accepts changes once implemented before proceeding to subsequent project phases.

1.5 Final Delivery: Upon project completion and milestone sign-off, Customer will be provided with a professionally engineered, fully functional website adhering to modern web standards.

2. Scope of Work & Content Management Solutions

In Plain English

We build your online presence with a flexible Content Management System (CMS) so you can easily update text and media. Any custom features, plugins, or consulting outside the original quote are billed at our standard hourly rates.

2.1 Online Presence Development: For the initial phase of development, Sheernox will work closely with the Customer to establish the website design concept, brand styling, layout hierarchy, and core digital content.

2.2 Content Management System (CMS): Sheernox will configure and maintain an administrative Content Management Solution enabling Customer’s authorized personnel to securely add, edit, and update web pages and media. All standard commercial extensions and plugins required for core CMS functionality will be procured and configured by Sheernox.

2.3 Out-of-Scope Hourly Consulting: Design, consulting, and development services outside the explicit Statement of Work, estimate, or invoice will be considered additional scope and billed on an hourly basis at Sheernox’s prevailing professional design and engineering rates.

3. Technical Specifications & Developer Prerogative

In Plain English

To ensure system stability, security, and performance, Sheernox retains full technical discretion over code architecture, database relationships, programming languages, and UI implementation choices.

3.1 Site Architecture & Administrative CMS: The website will consist of multiple pages organized with persistent navigation to allow end-users to intuitively browse company services. The administrative CMS will consist of secure, authenticated web pages where Customer’s designated personnel can manage site assets.

3.2 Exclusive Technical Discretion: All technical decisions regarding design and programming techniques, programming languages, software technologies, architecture strategy, logic flows, font licensing/selection, database setup, schema relationships, visual layout (e.g. form fields, buttons, spacing, styling), and implementation details designed to meet the Customer’s documented needs remain the exclusive prerogative of Sheernox Technology Group and not that of the Customer.

4. Web Hosting & External File Transfer Fee ($250 CAD)

In Plain English

Websites are optimized to run on Sheernox cloud infrastructure. If you choose to host on an external 3rd-party provider, a $250 CAD packaging fee applies to prepare database and CMS export packages, and Sheernox is fully relieved of hosting warranties and deployment liabilities.

4.1 Integrated Sheernox Infrastructure: Hosting is coordinated directly by Sheernox’s technical team in tandem with website development to guarantee uptime, speed, security patching, and automated backups. Domain registration and SSL certificates may be bundled.

4.2 External 3rd-Party Hosting & $250 CAD File Fee: The website project will be hosted on Sheernox cloud infrastructure by default. If Customer elects to host elsewhere, Sheernox cannot accept responsibility for hardware, software dependencies, installation, functionality, or reliability on external systems. Hosting elsewhere automatically incurs a mandatory $250.00 CAD file transfer fee.

Clarification: This fee is not a penalty, but compensates engineering labor required to convert the bespoke CMS, databases, media libraries, themes, and configuration files into an archival transport package (such as an encrypted ZIP archive or cPanel backup package) ready for third-party server importation.

4.3 Warranty Void on External Servers: Hosting on a third-party server automatically voids all performance warranties and relieves Sheernox from any and all liability, including lost files, database corruption, downtime, or expenses incurred by Customer during or after migration. Sheernox is not responsible for deploying, installing, or troubleshooting the website on third-party servers.

5. Review, Revisions & 10-Day Sign-off

In Plain English

Milestones include up to two consolidated rounds of minor revisions. You have 10 business days to review deliverables and provide written feedback; silence beyond 10 days constitutes milestone acceptance so projects remain on schedule.

5.1 Revision Rounds: Unless otherwise specified in a Statement of Work, each visual development milestone includes up to two (2) consolidated rounds of styling, copy placement, and layout revisions.

5.2 10-Day Deemed Acceptance: Customer agrees to review staging deliverables and submit written feedback or sign-off within ten (10) business days of milestone presentation. Failure to provide feedback within ten (10) business days constitutes constructive acceptance of that milestone.

6. Website Care & Ongoing Maintenance Plans

In Plain English

Website maintenance is NOT included in web design packages by default. If you are not subscribed to an active Website Care plan, you are responsible for CMS updates and security maintenance post-launch. Ad-hoc fixes are billed per incident.

6.1 Maintenance Not Default: Website care and ongoing maintenance are NOT included with web design packages by default. Dedicated Website Care plans (covering regular core updates, security patches, extension updates, and automated off-site backups) are offered separately under monthly or annual retainers.

6.2 Customer Responsibility for Unmanaged Sites: Customer agrees that if they are not subscribed to an active Website Care plan, Customer is solely responsible for all maintenance, plugin updates, security hardening, database optimization, and content upkeep once the site is delivered. Subsequent maintenance or repair requests will be billed per incident at standard hourly rates.

7.1 Ownership of Client Materials: Customer warrants that all text, photos, logos, trademarks, and artwork provided to Sheernox for inclusion are owned by Customer or properly licensed. Customer shall indemnify and hold Sheernox harmless from any third-party copyright, trademark, or intellectual property claims arising from supplied materials.

7.2 Stock Photography & Font Licenses: Commercial stock photography, typefaces, and third-party APIs procured for the project are licensed according to their vendor terms. Recurring license fees remain the responsibility of Customer.

8. Copyright Transfer, Ownership & Criminal Code Protection

In Plain English

The website remains the property of Sheernox until paid in full. Once your invoices are fully settled, copyright automatically transfers to you. Leaching or taking files prior to full payment is treated as theft under the Criminal Code of Canada.

8.1 Intellectual Property Retention: The website design, code, graphics, and staging deliverables remain the exclusive intellectual property of Sheernox Technology Group until all project invoices are paid in full by the Customer.

8.2 Automatic Transfer Upon Full Payment: Following full and final settlement of all project invoices, intellectual property rights and copyright in the custom visual design, layout, and bespoke code are automatically transferred to Customer.

8.3 Criminal Code of Canada Protection: Any attempt to scrape, copy, leach, deploy, or take project deliverables from Sheernox servers prior to full payment will be considered criminal theft corresponding to Sections 322 through 380 of the Criminal Code of Canada (R.S.C., 1985, c. C-46) and pursued through law enforcement and civil litigation.

8.4 Portfolio & Marketing Rights: Sheernox reserves the professional right to showcase the completed website, screenshots, and case studies in its marketing materials, design portfolio, and agency promotional channels.

9. Payment Terms, Initiation Deposit, Late Fees & Recovery

In Plain English

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to start work. Invoices are net 30 days. Late balances accrue 1.8% monthly interest. Unpaid accounts may have site access disabled and will be pursued through the Small Claims Court of BC with full legal costs.

9.1 50% Non-Refundable Initiation Deposit: Customer shall pay Sheernox an initiation deposit equal to fifty percent (50%) of the project’s total estimate before any design or engineering work begins. The initiation deposit is non-refundable.

9.2 Balance Due Upon Completion: Once the deposit is paid and work is completed, Customer is obligated to pay the outstanding balance in full prior to public launch or handoff.

9.3 Net 30 Payment Terms & 1.8% Monthly Late Interest: Payment terms are net thirty (30) days from the date of invoice issuance. Any amounts not paid within thirty (30) days shall incur a late interest fee of 1.8% per month (23.87% per annum compounded monthly) for each month or portion thereof that payment remains outstanding.

9.4 Delinquency Remedies & Service Suspension: If accounts are not settled and Sheernox has not been contacted regarding delays, access to the related website may be denied and web pages removed from public view.

9.5 Legal Enforcement & Small Claims Court: Sheernox will refer delinquent accounts to the Small Claims Court of British Columbia or the Supreme Court of British Columbia to obtain payment. Unpaid judgments may be recorded against Customer's credit history. Following consistent non-payment, Sheernox's legal counsel will seek payment through court procedures, with Customer responsible for all collection expenses, court filing fees, and legal fees on a full solicitor-and-own-client indemnity basis.

10. Refund Policy & Dispute Administrative Fees

10.1 Non-Refundable Payments: All payments made to Sheernox Technology Group are strictly non-refundable. All overcharges or billing discrepancies must be reported within twenty-one (21) days of the transaction date.

10.2 Credit Card Dispute Fee ($250 – $500 CAD): If Customer disputes a valid charge with their credit card issuer or financial institution that, in Sheernox’s sole discretion, constitutes a legitimate charge under this Agreement or the Master Terms, Customer agrees to pay an Administrative Fee of not less than $250.00 CAD and not more than $500.00 CAD to cover investigation and dispute administration costs.

11. Cross-Browser, Device & Platform Standards

11.1 Evergreen Browsers: Websites are engineered to render reliably across evergreen releases of modern desktop and mobile browsers (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge).

11.2 Discontinued Engines: Sheernox expressly disclaims compatibility with obsolete, discontinued, or non-standard browsers, including Microsoft Internet Explorer, early legacy Trident/Presto engines, or unpatched mobile browsers.

12.1 Third-Party Disputes & Client Wrongdoing: Sheernox will not be liable for or become involved in disputes between the Customer (site owner) and Customer's end-users or clients, and cannot be held responsible for any wrongdoing on the part of a site owner.

12.2 Consequential Damages & Earnings Loss: Sheernox will not be liable for any costs incurred, compensation, or loss of earnings due to work carried out on behalf of Customer, or due to server unavailability, software defects, or downtime.

12.3 Security Vulnerabilities & Attack Indemnity: Sheernox is not liable for damages caused by security loopholes (such as compromised usernames or passwords), malicious software, unauthorized hacking, Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks, or malicious exploits. Sheernox is not liable for third-party theft of intellectual property or sensitive customer financial information. Any emergency work required to remediate security loopholes or repair damages is not the responsibility of Sheernox and will be billed separately.

12.4 Literary, Typographical & Content Disclaimers: Sheernox is not liable for damages resulting from spelling mistakes, poor grammar, sentence structure, paragraph formatting, legibility, literary logic, or any issue arising from the manner in which content provided or approved by Customer is written or displayed. Sheernox is not liable for damages caused by offensive images, poor quality images, missing images, excessive images, or any visual asset furnished by Customer.

12.5 Current Contact Information: Customer must provide and continuously maintain current, accurate contact information with Sheernox, including primary email address, mailing address, and telephone contacts.

12.6 Survival: This document in its entirety, including all limitation of liability, indemnity, copyright, and payment enforcement provisions, shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

13. Governing Law, Exclusive Jurisdiction & British Columbia Venue

In Plain English

This agreement is governed exclusively by the laws of British Columbia and Canada. Any legal action must be brought before the courts in Kamloops, BC. Making a payment constitutes legal acceptance of these terms.

13.1 British Columbia Governing Law & Venue: This Agreement shall be deemed to have been made in, and shall be construed pursuant to, the laws of the Province of British Columbia and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. Any legal action or proceeding arising out of or related to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the courts of British Columbia sitting in the City of Kamloops, British Columbia, Canada. The parties hereby consent to such exclusive jurisdiction and venue.

13.2 Implied Authority Upon Payment: The Customer’s agreement and legal execution of this document is implied the moment the Customer initiates an order, payment, or deposit to Sheernox Technology Group, pursuant to the British Columbia Electronic Transactions Act (SBC 2001, c. 10).

13.3 Taxes & Currency: All prices are stated in Canadian Dollars (CAD) unless explicitly designated otherwise in a formal Statement of Work. Applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) and British Columbia Provincial Sales Tax (PST) will be assessed in accordance with statutory regulations.
