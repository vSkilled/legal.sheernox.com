1.0 MASTER PROMOTIONAL TERMS & GENERAL RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The general provisions set forth in this Section 1.0 apply universally to all
promotional offerings, discounts, and special pricing published or extended by
Sheernox, except where a specific sub-promotion schedule explicitly provides
otherwise in writing.

Unless expressly authorized in writing by Sheernox, promotional offers,
automated checkout discounts, and promotional voucher codes cannot be combined,
stacked, or aggregated with any other promotion, affiliate incentive, volume
discount, agency pricing tier, or enterprise contract concession. If an order
qualifies for more than one automated or manual promotion, our billing
automation will apply only the single promotion yielding the highest nominal
savings to the customer.

All promotional pricing, percentage discounts, and introductory credits apply
strictly and exclusively to the initial contracted billing cycle or promotional
period specified in the service order (e.g., the first month of a monthly plan
or the first year of an annual plan).

Automatic Renewal at Standard Prevailing Rates: Upon the expiration of the
initial promotional billing cycle, all contracted services automatically renew
at Sheernox's standard, non-promotional recurring rate in effect at the time of
renewal. All figures are billed in Canadian Dollars (CAD) and are subject to
applicable federal Goods and Services Tax (GST, 5%) and British Columbia
Provincial Sales Tax (PST, 7%). Clients may cancel automatic renewal at any time
prior to the scheduled invoice date through the client management portal at
my.sheernox.com in accordance with Section 4.0 of our Master Terms.

Promotional rates are calculated based on the specific hosting plan, resource
tier, and billing cycle selected at the time of initial checkout. If a client
upgrades, downgrades, modifies billing cadences (e.g., switching from annual to
monthly), or transfers the service to another user account mid-cycle during a
promotional period:

- Immediate Forfeiture: The remaining balance of any promotional discount or
  credit is immediately and irrevocably forfeited.
- Standard Rate Recalculation: Billed charges for the modified service are
  recalculated based on Sheernox's standard prevailing non-promotional recurring
  rate, pro-rated for the remaining duration of the billing cycle.
- No Cash Value: Forfeited promotional credits or discounts possess zero cash
  surrender value and cannot be refunded, transferred, or credited to subsequent
  invoices.

Sheernox maintains a zero-tolerance policy against fraudulent activities and
manipulation designed to circumvent promotional rules. Unless a specific
promotion explicitly states a per-customer limit, clients in good standing may
place multiple eligible orders during an open promotional window.

However, the creation of fictitious, duplicate, or secondary accounts by the
same individual or entity, using temporary email providers, falsifying
registrant details, or coordinating abusive purchasing rings constitutes a
material breach of our Acceptable Use Policy. Sheernox reserves the unilateral
right to audit transactions, void fraudulent orders, cancel active services
without notice, and retroactively invoice the account for full standard
non-promotional service fees.

Where a promotional hosting service is subject to Sheernox's standard 30-day
money-back guarantee pursuant to our Master Terms:

- Net Amount Paid: Any approved refund shall be strictly limited to the actual
  net discounted amount paid by the customer for the affected hosting service.
- Non-Refundable Items: Third-party licensing fees, dedicated IP addresses,
  custom software setup charges, domain name registrations, and domain transfer
  fees are strictly non-refundable and will be deducted from any refund amount
  as detailed in Section 3.0.

Sheernox reserves the right to terminate, amend, withdraw, or modify any
promotional offering, discount tier, or promotional schedule at any time. Any
